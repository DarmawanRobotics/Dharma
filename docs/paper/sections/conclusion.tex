\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Dharma shows that a small autonomy stack can be robot-agnostic and explicit about its
assumptions: one robot profile feeds every limit, the localization estimate carries its
uncertainty and gates its corrections, and one braking model bounds every speed, from the route
profile to a protective field that treats missing data as an obstacle. Pure cores and enforced
conventions keep these models consistent across eleven repositories and two ROS~2
distributions. In simulation the field kept its margin and the gated filter cut the
localization error sixfold; the same runs exposed a path-shift deadlock and a follower blind to
a held robot. Future work is a pose graph, deskewed near-field perception and a second robot
model on the same stack.
