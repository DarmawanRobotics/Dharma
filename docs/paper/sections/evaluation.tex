\textbf{Protocol.} E1--E4 close the loop over the pure cores at the node rates: the simulator
core (acceleration limits, collisions, Mid-360 ray casting, odometry drift
$\vect{\xi} \sim \mathcal{N}(\vect{0}, \boldsymbol{\Sigma}\,\Delta s)$, 50/20~ms sensor/command
latency), perception, the follower with VFH and the mux, all with package defaults and ideal
localization except in E3. Each condition runs seeds 1--20 (mean $\pm$ std);
\texttt{experiments/} in the hub reruns them and regenerates the tables.

\textbf{E1: stopping distance.} At speed $v$ a person appears on the robot's axis $D(v) + m$
ahead, the last point from which \eqref{eq:braking} promises a stop with the margin. From the
first scan showing it, the robot stopped after \EOneMeasuredA, \EOneMeasuredB, \EOneMeasuredC{}
and \EOneMeasuredD~m (std $\le$ \EOneStdMax~m) at 0.2--0.8~m/s, against $D(v) =$
\EOneModelA, \EOneModelB, \EOneModelC{} and \EOneModelD~m; in \EOneRuns{} runs the clearance
never fell below \EOneClearMin~m and braking began \EOneReactMin--\EOneReactMax~s after the
appearance, inside $\tr$; the body brakes at exactly $a$ here.

\textbf{E2: blind LiDAR.} At 0.5~m/s the LiDAR delivers clouds without points or with 50
points for 4~s: v0.2 commanded zero within \ETwoBlindZeroMax~s and stood within
\ETwoBlindDistanceMax~m; v0.1, re-implemented as a baseline (an all-zero blind scan that its field skipped
as free), drove on at full speed. A silent LiDAR stopped both after the 0.5~s timeout
(at most \ETwoSilentZeroMax~s). In REMOTE v0.2 crept at \ETwoCreepMax~m/s, v0.1 kept
0.5~m/s.

\textbf{E3: localization.} The follower drives three laps (\EThreeDistance~m) of the
simulator's mall route with its drift scenario (1\,\% scale, $0.1^\circ$/m yaw bias) past
four tags, detected at 5~Hz with the noise the tag source assumes plus 5\,\% outliers
(5--25$^\circ$, 0.1--0.4~m); all estimators get the same inputs (Table~\ref{tab:loc}). Hard
replacement rejected \EThreeHardReplaceInliersRejected\,\% of the good detections, as its
0.5~m threshold on \frm{map}$\to$\frm{odom} grows with the distance from the odometry origin;
the gate rejected \EThreeEkfTagsOutliersRejected\,\% of the outliers and
\EThreeEkfTagsInliersRejected\,\% of the good detections.

\textbf{E4: path shift.} The follower drives 20~m along a 3~m corridor past a person standing on
the route (\emph{on}) or two beside it, one walking ahead and one crossing; nobody gives way
(Table~\ref{tab:shift}). Path shift passed in 37 of 40 runs; reactive avoidance never passed a
person on the route, as the guard stops where the VFH emergency cone holds the robot and the
VFH gap heading ignores the body width. The failures expose defects: path shift deadlocks when it stops
within its stop margin before reaching its offset; the follower reports \textsc{following}
while the mux field holds the robot; a walking person once brushed the stopped robot, as the
field ignores the speed of people~\cite{isots15066}.
